Purpose & Problem Statement

Many students today are unaware of the dangers lurking online — from phishing scams and cyberbullying to identity theft and data privacy violations. Schools rarely teach practical digital safety skills, and parents often don't know how to protect their children online.

SafeKids solves this by providing an engaging, gamified cybersecurity education app. Through daily tips, quizzes, and real-world scenarios, students learn how to protect themselves — and parents get peace of mind with family safety tools.

⭐

3 Main Features

FEATURE 01

🧠 Daily Cyber Tips & Lessons

Every day, students receive a bite-sized cybersecurity lesson covering topics like password safety, phishing, social media privacy, and OTP scams. Lessons are written for easy understanding with real-life examples.

FEATURE 02

🎮 Gamified Safety Quizzes

Interactive quizzes turn cybersecurity learning into a game. Students earn points, badges, and unlock levels as they answer questions correctly. A leaderboard encourages friendly competition among classmates.

FEATURE 03

🚨 Threat Scenario Simulator

Students face simulated real-world scenarios — a phishing email, a scam call, an OTP request — and must choose the right action. This teaches critical thinking in a safe environment before they encounter real threats.

✨

Additional Features

BONUS 01

👨‍👩‍👧 Parent Dashboard

Parents can track their child's progress, set screen time reminders, and receive alerts when risky online behaviour patterns are detected.

BONUS 02

📊 Safety Score

A personal cybersecurity score that improves as students complete lessons, quizzes, and good digital habits — motivating consistent learning.

BONUS 03

🆘 Emergency Helpline

Quick-access button to report cyberbullying, contact school counsellors, or reach national cybercrime helplines with a single tap.

Development Roadmap

PHASE 2

MVP Development (Week 3–5)

Build core features using MIT App Inventor: daily tips, basic quiz, user login.

PHASE 3

Testing & Feedback (Week 6)

PHASE 4

Advanced Features (Week 7–8)

Add scenario simulator, leaderboard, parent dashboard, and safety score.
