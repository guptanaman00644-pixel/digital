🔑 Key Safety Areas

🔑

Password Safety

Your password is your first line of defence online.

  • Use at least 12 characters
  • Mix letters, numbers, and symbols
  • Use a different password for each site
  • Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)
  • Don't use your name or birthday
  • Never share passwords with friends
🎣

Phishing Awareness

Phishing tricks you into giving up your personal information.

  • Check the sender's email address carefully
  • Never click suspicious links in emails or SMS
  • Look for HTTPS in website URLs
  • Verify requests from "banks" by calling them
  • Don't open attachments from strangers
  • Urgency in emails is a red flag
🌐

Safe Browsing

Browse smart to protect your data and privacy.

  • Use trusted, secure websites (HTTPS)
  • Keep your browser & OS updated
  • Avoid public Wi-Fi for sensitive tasks
  • Use a VPN when on public networks
  • Don't download from unknown sources
  • Never ignore security warnings
📱

Social Media Privacy

Protect your identity and reputation online.

  • Set profiles to private
  • Be careful what you post publicly
  • Don't share your location in real time
  • Think before you tag others
  • Don't accept friend requests from strangers
  • Avoid oversharing personal details
📲

OTP & Scam Awareness

One-Time Passwords (OTPs) and financial scams are on the rise.

  • Never share OTPs with anyone — even "banks"
  • No legitimate service asks for OTP via call
  • Verify UPI requests before paying
  • Report suspicious calls to authorities
  • Don't click payment links in SMS
  • Too-good-to-be-true offers are scams
💻

Device Security

Keep your devices healthy and protected.

  • Install a trusted antivirus software
  • Regularly update your operating system
  • Lock your screen with a PIN or biometric
  • Back up important data regularly
  • Don't use cracked/pirated software
  • Never leave devices unlocked in public

✅ Do's & ❌ Don'ts

✅ DO These Things

  • Use strong, unique passwords everywhere
  • Enable 2FA on all important accounts
  • Keep software updated
  • Report cyberbullying and scams
  • Think before you click any link
  • Log out from shared computers
  • Read privacy policies carefully

❌ DON'T Do These

  • Share passwords with anyone
  • Click unknown links or pop-ups
  • Download apps from unofficial stores
  • Post personal info publicly online
  • Use the same password everywhere
  • Trust calls asking for OTP or money
  • Connect to unsecured public Wi-Fi
